icanhaz — an interactive terminal to your machine, in the browser, over wRPC

xterm.js ⟷ the wRPC streaming client (openTerminal) ⟷ icanhazd ⟷ a real PTY running your login shell. Keystrokes and output are stream<u8> sub-channels multiplexed over one connection. Prefers WebTransport (paste the daemon's cert hash) and falls back to WebSocket. Run icanhazd, then vite, and click below. (Live resize is a TODO — the PTY is fixed at 80×24.)
